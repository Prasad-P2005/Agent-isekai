Everything that has changed since the last update on 24 October is now on main. The biggest change is how the whole world looks and moves.

NEW
■New character art. Every person and creature is redrawn as a chunky Minecraft-style skin, with mixed body types, cute elf girls, majestic kings and mermaids with long tails.
■A really big dragon. About 25 blocks across the wings. One lives in each open land of the Dragonspine Peaks and circles its lair, well away from the Law Stones.
■More magic. Frost Nova, Chain Lightning, Blink, Mana Ward and Meteor. Your mana well deepens at a temple with Mana Crystals, and slowly with every spell.
■A compass. It runs along the top of the screen and shows which way you face and where everything you are after lies.
■A first-person hand. It stays in the corner of the view and does the work: mining, punching, placing blocks and throwing spells.
CHANGED
■Movement feels smoother. You now speed up and slow down instead of starting and stopping dead, and the camera and creatures glide at any refresh rate.
■No AI service needed. The story, the people and every line of dialogue are written and shipped with the game, so it runs without a key.
■A new interface. Menus are now pages of one book, with a slate dock and bag for what your hands use.
■Easier setup. The README has copy-paste steps for Docker, Windows and macOS or Linux.
FIXED
■Ledges. Walking up onto a one-block ledge no longer snaps you to the top instantly. The view now rises smoothly.
■Flickering. Faces and joints no longer shimmer where two surfaces overlapped.
STILL TO CHECK
■Not tested yet. The automated tests and a full play-through have not been run on this build, so expect some rough edges.
