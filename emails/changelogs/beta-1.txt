Isekai Realms Beta 1: the world wakes up
31 October 2026 | Beta 1

The world is bigger, its creatures think for themselves, and something very old now sleeps under a black mountain in the east.

NEW
- The Wyrm. One dragon in all the world, asleep in a cave under the Wyrmcrown. No blade can mark it until the endgame. Wake it and it chases you off the mountain, breathing fire, and it remembers you.
- A bigger world. 10 by 10 lands, up from 8 by 8, with the drowned Mirefen in the south and two new villages.
- Ten new creatures. Deer, boars, crabs, bats, bandits, skeleton archers, cave spiders, mountain trolls and ashen wraiths, with shoals of fish in the water.
- A bestiary. The journal has an entry for every creature, unlocked the first time you get a good look at it.
- More to do. Five new guild quests, a longer board as your rank rises, more favors, and a final hunt the King offers once you are Oathbound.

CHANGED
- Spawning like Minecraft. Packs by biome and by light, caves with their own monsters, and lanterns that truly keep the dark things away. Nothing appears where you are looking.
- Despawning that makes sense. Creatures leave when they are far off and idle, never in front of you. Named foes track you down again.
- Real pathfinding. A shared flow field for the hunt and A* for errands, so creatures go round walls and through doors.
- Smarter by nature. Wolves encircle you, goblins run when hurt, skeletons keep their range, spiders pounce, deer bolt as a herd and guards call for help.
- Grand town buildings. The guild hall, smithy, hospital, inn, church and keep are rebuilt from the ground up, with real interiors.

FIXED
- Empty shops. Blacksmiths, guild masters and doctors now keep their posts day and night, even in the middle of a big city.
- Wall hopping. Characters only jump at a one-block step, not at every wall they meet.
- Lost events. Raids, caravans and the law wait for somewhere to stand instead of quietly failing.

STILL TO CHECK
- Not played yet. This build has not been run through the automated tests or played in a browser, so expect rough edges, especially in the new art and the dragon fight. Old saves upgrade themselves, but the far east and south coasts will change shape.
